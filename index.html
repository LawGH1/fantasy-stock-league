How it works

Three steps, one week

1

Draft

Pick five tokenized stocks from the Robinhood Chain registry. Twelve budget points. Nvidia and SpaceX cost four, gold and the S&P cost one. Entries close Sunday night.

2

Score

Monday open to Friday close. Each pick scores its percent move from the on-chain pool price. Your team score is the average. Leaderboard updates every 15 minutes.

3

Get paid

Friday after the close, the top ten teams split the pot. Payouts go out from the league wallet as public transactions you can check on the explorer.

Prizes

Top ten split the pot

The pot is every entry for the week, plus whatever the league seeds. The split is fixed, so you know what a finish is worth before you enter.

A slice of each pot buys back FSL. Amounts and links are posted after every event.

Where it stands
  • Draft engine. Live prices, budget, five-pick teams. Running on this page.
  • Scoring. Percent moves from on-chain pool prices, recomputable by anyone. Code is public.
  • Entries. An entry is a transfer of FSL to the league wallet. Opens with the token.
  • Payouts. Sent from the league wallet after each event, posted with transaction links.
  • Draft the traders. Adding the chain's top wallets as picks, scored by their PnL. After the first events run.
