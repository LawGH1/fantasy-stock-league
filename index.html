CHAPTER 02
How a round plays out

Sixty minutes. Then it starts again.

No apps to install, no accounts to make. A portfolio, one hour of price moves, and a payout. Twenty-four rounds a day.

Any time

Enter

Build a portfolio and send HOURLY to the prize wallet. You're in the next round. Enter at 2:40, you play the 3:00 round.

:00 · Open

Prices recorded

At the top of the hour the pool price of every stock in your portfolio is locked in. That's your starting line. No changes after this.

:00 to :59

Live scoring

Each pick scores its percent move from the open. Your portfolio's score is the average of five. The leaderboard refreshes every minute.

:00 · Close

Payday, then the next round

The top of the next hour locks the table. The top ten are paid from the prize wallet within minutes, and the same tick opens the next round.

CHAPTER 04
The rulebook

Short enough to read. Strict enough to trust.

1

Five picks, twelve points

Every stock has a fixed cost from one to four points, set for the whole season. You cannot go over budget and you cannot enter with fewer than five.

2

Prices come from the chain

Each stock's score uses its deepest Uniswap pool on Robinhood Chain, read from public trackers. No judges, no oracles you have to trust, and no price we can quietly change.

3

Open to close

Start is the pool price at the top of the hour. End is the top of the next hour. Score per pick is the percent change. Portfolio score is the plain average of five. Rounds run around the clock, weekends included, because the pools never close.

4

Enter as many portfolios as you like

Each portfolio is its own entry and its own shot at the top ten. Hold the holder threshold of HOURLY and one portfolio an hour is free.

5

Locked means locked

Once a round opens, no swaps. Enter after :00 and you're in the next round, not this one. Ties go to the portfolio that entered first.

6

Everything is checkable

The prize wallet is public, so the pot is visible all round. The scoring code is public, so anyone can rerun a round and get the same table. Payouts are automatic and every one is a transaction link, not a promise.

Not available to US persons, in line with the stock tokens Hourly is scored on. Entry price and holder threshold are fixed for Season one and don't change mid-round.

CHAPTER 05
Prizes

Top ten split the pot.

The pot is every entry for the round plus whatever Hourly seeds. The split is fixed, so you know what each finish is worth before you enter. Ten places paid means a decent hour gets something back, not just a perfect one.

A slice of every pot buys back HOURLY on the open market. Amounts and transaction links are posted after each round, twenty-four times a day.
