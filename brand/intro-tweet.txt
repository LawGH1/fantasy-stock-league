Introducing Hourly on Robinhood Chain.

Robinhood put real stocks on chain. Nvidia, Tesla, SpaceX, gold, GameStop, all trading 24/7 as tokens. Nobody built a game on top of them. We did.

How it works:

Draft five stocks under a 12-point budget. Big names cost 4, quiet ones cost 1.

At the top of every hour a round opens and the on-chain pool price of each stock in your portfolio is recorded. Sixty minutes later it closes. Each stock scores its percent move, your score is the average of five.

The top ten portfolios split the pot. Payouts go out from the prize wallet within minutes, and the next round opens on the same tick. 24 rounds a day, weekends included, because the pools never close.

The tech:

Prices come straight from the deepest Uniswap pool for each stock token on Robinhood Chain. No oracle, no judge, no number we can change.

Entries are a transfer of $HOURLY to a public prize wallet. Payouts are transactions from it. The scoring code is public so anyone can rerun a round and get the same table.

$HOURLY is the game's chips. 85% of every entry goes to the pot, 10% buys $HOURLY back from the pool, 5% runs Hourly. Hold the threshold and one portfolio an hour is free.

Launching on Pons with locked liquidity. No team allocation beyond a small published reserve, locked on chain for three months.

The draft and the wheel are live now. No wallet needed to try it.
