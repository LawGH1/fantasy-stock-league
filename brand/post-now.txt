Introducing Hourly on Robinhood Chain.

Robinhood put real stocks on chain. Nvidia, Tesla, SpaceX, gold, GameStop, all trading 24/7 as tokens. Nobody built a game on top of them. We did.

Draft five stocks under a 12-point budget. At the top of every hour a round opens and the on-chain price of each stock is recorded. Sixty minutes later it closes. Percent move per stock, average of five, top ten split the pot. Paid within minutes. 24 rounds a day.

Prices come from the deepest Uniswap pool for each stock. No oracle, no judge. Entries are a transfer of $HOURLY to a public league wallet. Payouts are transactions from it. The scoring code is public.

85% of every entry goes to the pot. 10% buys $HOURLY back. 5% runs the league.

The draft and the wheel are live now. No wallet needed.

https://fantasystockleague.app

CA and first round time will be posted here. Not available to US persons. Not financial advice.
